Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Money in Blocks ("we", "us") sells downloadable budgeting templates at moneyinblocks.com and publishes short educational videos about everyday money. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Who is responsible for your data

Money in Blocks is run by Ignacio Pedro Soria Kowarik, an individual based in Almería, Spain, who acts as the data controller.

Contact: hello@moneyinblocks.com

What we collect

  • When you buy: your name, email address, country, what you bought, the price and taxes paid. Payments are handled by Stripe or PayPal through our store platform, Payhip. We never see or store your full card number.
  • When you contact us: your email address and whatever you write to us.
  • If you join our email list (only if you choose to): your email address and whether you open our emails.
  • When you visit the store: basic technical data (such as IP address, browser and pages visited) collected by Payhip to run the store securely. See Payhip's cookie and privacy notices for details.

We don't collect anything you type into the budgeting template. The template runs in your own Google Sheets or Excel file, and we can't see it.

Why we use it (and our legal basis)

  • To deliver your purchase and give support (performance of a contract).
  • To keep invoices and tax records (legal obligation).
  • To prevent fraud and keep the store secure (legitimate interest).
  • To send you emails about new templates or videos, only if you opted in (consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link).

We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Only with the services we need to run the business:

  • Payhip (store and file delivery, United Kingdom)
  • Stripe and PayPal (payment processing)
  • Our email provider, if you join the list
  • Tax authorities, when the law requires it

Some of these providers process data outside the European Economic Area (for example in the UK or the US). These transfers are protected by adequacy decisions or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Purchase and tax records: for as long as Spanish tax and accounting law requires (generally up to 6 years).
  • Support emails: up to 2 years.
  • Email list data: until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@moneyinblocks.com and we'll reply within one month.

If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, www.aepd.es) or to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our store is not intended for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Important changes will be announced on the store.
