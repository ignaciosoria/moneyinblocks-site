How it works

Two minutes, every payday.

Your bank shows one number. The template splits it into the money that's already promised and the money that's actually yours.

  • Type what you haveYour balance, your next payday and how often you get paid: weekly, every two weeks, twice a month or monthly.
  • Add your blocksOnly the bills due before payday, a slice for yearly costs, groceries and gas, and savings first.
  • Read one numberWhat's free to spend until payday, and per day. If the plan doesn't fit, it tells you by how much.
