How it works

Two minutes, every payday.

Your bank shows one number. The sheet splits it into the money that's already promised and the money that's actually yours.

1

Right now

Type the money in your account, your next payday and how often you get paid.

3

Your number

Questions

Before you buy

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. You type your numbers in. Nothing is connected, nothing is shared, and we never see what you enter.

I get paid every two weeks. Does it work?

Yes. It works with weekly, every-two-weeks, twice-a-month and monthly paychecks, and only counts the bills that are due before your next one.

Do I need Excel?

No. You get a Google Sheets version (free with any Google account) and an Excel file. Use whichever you like.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, in the Google Sheets app. It's built so the number you need is at the top.

What if something doesn't work?

Email hello@moneyinblocks.com within 30 days and we'll fix it, replace it or refund you.

Is this financial advice?

No. It's an educational planning tool. The result is only as accurate as the numbers you type in.
